Quantitative Policy Assessment with MOCAT-MC

Scenario-based orbital population modeling used to examine how mitigation choices change long-term sustainability outcomes.

Research question

How can orbital population models support comparison of debris-mitigation strategies?

The project uses computational scenarios to connect measurable changes in the orbital environment with policy choices rather than treating mitigation as an abstract recommendation.

Method

Compare intervention scenarios over time.

01Population baseline
02Policy / mitigation scenario
03Monte Carlo evolution
04Outcome comparison

MOCAT-MC scenarios establish baseline orbital populations and test changes in assumptions or mitigation behavior. Resulting population, debris, and risk patterns can then be compared across scenarios.

Why it matters

Connecting technical evidence to decisions.

Quantitative policy assessment makes the consequences of mitigation choices more explicit and creates a stronger basis for comparing interventions before they are implemented.
