Societal Impacts of AI for Space Decision-Making

A developing research direction examining how model choice and framing can change policy-facing recommendations.

Research question

How does the AI system we choose change the policy advice we receive?

If different models interpret identical technical evidence differently, model selection can quietly become part of the policy process itself.

Method

Hold evidence constant and compare interpretation.

01Shared evidence
02Multiple AI models
03Reasoning comparison
04Governance implications

The research direction compares responses from multiple AI systems using the same technical evidence and policy questions, then examines differences in assumptions, uncertainty, emphasis, and recommendations.

My contribution

Where I contribute to the work.

Question design

Building comparable policy prompts around fixed technical evidence.

Model comparison

Identifying meaningful differences in reasoning and recommendation structure.

Uncertainty analysis

Examining how confidently models state claims relative to the evidence.

Governance interpretation

Assessing what model variation means for accountable decision support.

Why it matters

Connecting technical evidence to decisions.

High-consequence decision systems need evaluation that goes beyond factual accuracy to include framing, uncertainty, consistency, and the institutional effects of model choice.
