Research question

Can AI translate orbital simulation outputs into policy insight without obscuring uncertainty?

Catalyst focuses on the layer between technical evidence and institutional decisions: the point where complex outputs have to become interpretable enough to support action.

Method

A simulation-to-reasoning pipeline.

01MOCAT-MC scenarios
02Structured indicators
03LLM reasoning
04Policy interpretation

Scenario outputs are organized into interpretable indicators and provided to an LLM-based reasoning layer. The analysis can then be evaluated for consistency, framing sensitivity, explanation quality, and treatment of uncertainty.

My contribution

Where I contribute to the work.

Evaluation design

Comparing how reasoning changes with evidence, framing, or model choice.

Research communication

Preserving technical meaning across disciplinary boundaries.

Why it matters

Connecting technical evidence to decisions.

AI systems will increasingly sit between technical evidence and human decisions. The research question is what gets lost, amplified, or reframed in that translation.
