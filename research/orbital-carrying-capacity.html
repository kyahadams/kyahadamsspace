Research question

How can a physical limit in the orbital environment become a useful economic and policy concept?

The project treats carrying capacity as more than an environmental threshold. It asks how increasing congestion changes access, risk, and the value of operating in specific orbital shells.

Method

Connect orbital conditions to system-level consequence.

01Orbital shell definition
02Population / congestion metrics
03Risk & access interpretation
04Economic / policy implications

The analysis uses shell-level orbital conditions and sustainability indicators to examine how congestion and collision risk can constrain future access. The goal is a framework that can connect physical conditions to choices made by operators and institutions.

Why it matters

Connecting technical evidence to decisions.

A usable carrying-capacity concept could give decision-makers a common language for discussing when growth in an orbital region begins to create material costs for other users.
